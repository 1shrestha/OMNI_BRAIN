The pipeline

Three steps, same order, every time.

This is exactly what just ran above — no shortcuts, no black box.

01

Scrape

OmniBrain fetches the page or document and pulls out the real content — text, structure, and context — while stripping the noise around it.

02

Embed

Content is split into chunks and converted into vectors, stored so anything relevant can be found in milliseconds, not skimmed by hand.

03

Chat

Ask a question in plain language. OmniBrain finds the relevant chunks and answers grounded in what it actually read — not a guess.
